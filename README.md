# CyberSafe Lab 🛡️  
An interactive cybersecurity education platform for students and beginners.

## 🌐 Overview  
CyberSafe Lab is a hands-on learning environment designed to teach digital safety and ethical hacking fundamentals. It includes **9 interactive modules** that simulate real-world scenarios, helping users build practical skills in a safe and guided way.

## 🎯 Key Modules  
1. **Password Strength Checker** — Learn how to create strong, secure passwords.  
2. **Phishing Simulator** — Practice identifying suspicious emails and links.  
3. **2FA & Account Security Trainer** — Understand how two-factor authentication protects accounts.  
4. **Data Breach Awareness Simulator** — Explore the impact of data leaks and how to respond.  
5. **Social Engineering Awareness Trainer** — Recognize manipulation tactics and protect against human-based attacks.  
6. **HTTPS & Safe Browsing Trainer** — Learn why HTTPS matters and how to browse safely.  
7. **Public Wi-Fi & Network Safety Trainer** — Discover risks of open networks and how to stay secure.  
8. **Malware & Threat Awareness Dashboard** — Identify common malware types and understand basic defense strategies.  
9. **Research & Cybersecurity Learning Hub** — Access curated resources to continue learning and practicing cybersecurity.

## 👨‍💻 Who is it for?  
- Students and beginners interested in cybersecurity  
- Schools and educators looking for practical teaching tools  
- Anyone who wants to improve their digital safety

## 🚀 How to Use  
1. Clone the repository  
2. Run locally or deploy to a platform like Vercel  
3. Explore modules and test your cybersecurity awareness
